    Functional requirements

    What a digital system supporting MEASLES.A and MEASLES.B must do, each tied to its persona, user scenario, process step and source.

    id requirement persona user scenario process step source
    MEASLES.FR.01 The system shall show a client’s documented MCV doses with date and vaccine and how each counts (MCV0 MCV1 MCV2 invalid not counted) Health worker S1 S2 S3 MEASLES.A A2-A3 catchup.confirm-by-written-record; DT.03
    MEASLES.FR.02 The system shall treat a client with no documented MCV dose as unvaccinated Health worker S2 MEASLES.A A3 catchup.unconfirmed-assume-unvaccinated
    MEASLES.FR.03 The system shall determine the MCV dose due today from age and valid doses and the national schedule setting Health worker S1 S2 S3 MEASLES.A A3 DT.01; catchup.delayed-dose-no-restart; catchup.no-restart-series
    MEASLES.FR.04 The system shall not limit MCV1 or MCV2 to the target ages and shall offer missed doses at any contact Health worker S2 MEASLES.A A3 measles.routine-delivery-not-age-limited; measles.catch-up-every-opportunity; catchup.every-touchpoint; catchup.every-immunization-contact; catchup.late-not-denied
    MEASLES.FR.05 The system shall record any MCV dose given before 9 months as MCV0 and keep MCV1 and MCV2 due Health worker S3 MEASLES.A A7 measles.mcv0-recording; measles.mcv0-then-routine-doses
    MEASLES.FR.06 The system shall never record a dose as MCV2 for a client without a valid MCV1 Health worker; Data manager S5 MEASLES.A A7; MEASLES.B B4 catchup.mcv2-only-after-mcv1
    MEASLES.FR.07 The system shall prompt the screening questions (allergy to a component; severe immunosuppression; pregnancy; other live vaccine in the last 4 weeks) before vaccination Health worker S1 S4 MEASLES.A A5 DT.02
    MEASLES.FR.08 The system shall record the reason a client was not vaccinated and the return date Health worker S4 MEASLES.A A9 measles.avoid-in-pregnancy
    MEASLES.FR.09 The system shall calculate the next MCV due date and print it for the home-based record Health worker; Client S1 S2 MEASLES.A A7 catchup.multi-dose-return-date
    MEASLES.FR.10 The system shall flag the additional dose for an HIV-infected child on HAART Health worker — MEASLES.A A3 DT.04
    MEASLES.FR.11 The system shall list children overdue for MCV1 or MCV2 and zero-dose children each month Programme manager S5 MEASLES.B B1 catchup.defaulter-tracking
    MEASLES.FR.12 The system shall compute IND.01 to IND.07 and produce the Joint Reporting Form measles rows Data manager S5 MEASLES.B B4-B7 JRF 2016
    MEASLES.FR.13 The system shall record a health worker’s proof of measles immunity or immunization Programme manager S4 — measles.health-worker-immunity
