Data on vaccine effectiveness, antibody persistence, and modelling on long-term seroprotection indicate that an off-label, single dose schedule is equivalent to the two-dose schedule in children, in addition to being less costly and easier to implement. • For adults aged >40 years, vaccination with inactivated vaccines using the 2-dose schedule is preferred since there is insufficient evidence on the immunogenicity and long-term protection from a single dose in this age group. • Inactivated hepatitis A vaccines produced by different manufacturers, including combined hepatitis A vaccines, are interchangeable. • For immunocompromised individuals, until further experience has been obtained with a single- dose schedule, a 2-dose schedule of inactivated vaccine is recommended.
