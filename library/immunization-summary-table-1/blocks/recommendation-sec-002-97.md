From a public health perspective, the use of a single dose schedule can offer substantial benefits that outweigh the potential risk of a lower level of protection if efficacy wanes over time, although there is no current evidence of this. • Individuals known to be immunocompromised or HIV-infected (regardless of age or antiretroviral therapy status) should receive at least two HPV vaccine doses (minimum 6 months interval) and, where possible, three doses. • HPV vaccines can be co-administered with other non-live and live vaccines using separate syringes and different injection sites.
