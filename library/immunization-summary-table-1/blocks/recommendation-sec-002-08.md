Record (2025, 22:193–218) • Given the global burden of severe RSV disease among young infants, WHO recommends that all countries introduce products for the prevention of severe RSV disease in this population. • Decisions to use maternal RSV vaccination and/or a long-acting RSV monoclonal antibody (mAb) should consider factors such as cost, cost-effectiveness, financing, supply, anticipated coverage and feasibility of implementation within the existing health system. • WHO recommends the use of either the maternal RSV vaccine or a long-acting mAb, but not both, for the same mother-infant pair.
