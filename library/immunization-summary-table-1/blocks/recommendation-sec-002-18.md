For neonates with HIV infection confirmed by early virological testing, BCG vaccination should be delayed until ART has been started and the infant confirmed to be immunologically stable (CD4 >25%). • Moderate-to-late preterm infants (gestational age > 31 weeks) and low birth weight infants (< 2500 g) who are healthy and clinically stable can receive BCG vaccination at birth, or at the latest, upon discharge.
