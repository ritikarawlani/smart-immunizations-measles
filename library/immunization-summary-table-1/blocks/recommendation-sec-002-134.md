WHO prequalifies multiple conjugate vaccines and recommends countries select based on programmatic suitability, following product-specific guidance. • Meningococcal polysaccharide vaccines are less, or not, immunogenic in children under 2 years of age. • Meningococcal polysaccharide vaccines can be used for those ≥ 2 years of age to control outbreaks in countries where limited economic resources or insufficient supply restrict the use of meningococcal conjugate vaccines.
