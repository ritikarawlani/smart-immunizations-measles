The need for a booster dose in endemic settings has not been clearly established for any of the vaccines listed below: ▪ Inactivated Vero cell-derived vaccine: Primary series according to manufacturer’s recommendations (these vary by product), generally 2 doses at 4-week intervals starting the primary series at ≥6 months of age in endemic settings ▪ Live attenuated vaccine: Single dose administered at ≥8 months of age ▪ Live recombinant vaccine: Single dose administered at ≥9 months of age • Preferably, inactivated mouse brain-derived vaccines should be replaced by the newer generation JE vaccines discussed in this position paper.
