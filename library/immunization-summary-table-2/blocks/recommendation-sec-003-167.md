Having a strong influenza programme in place has been shown to be beneficial for the response to an influenza pandemic. • For countries considering the initiation or expansion of programmes for seasonal influenza vaccination, WHO recommends that the following target groups should be considered for vaccination (not in order of priority): health workers, individuals with comorbidities and underlying conditions, older adults and pregnant women. • Depending on national disease goals, capacity and resources, epidemiology, national policies and priorities, and disease burden, countries may consider additional (sub)populations for vaccination, such as children.
