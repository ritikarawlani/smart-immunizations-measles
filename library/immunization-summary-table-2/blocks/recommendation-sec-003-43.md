Ideally, there should be at least 4 years between booster doses. • Tetanus - To ensure lifelong protection against tetanus in all people should receive 6 doses (3 primary plus 3 booster doses) of tetanus toxoid-containing vaccine (TTCV) through routine childhood immunization schedules. • The 3 TTCV booster doses should be given at: 12–23 months of age; 4–7 years of age; and 9–15 years of age.
