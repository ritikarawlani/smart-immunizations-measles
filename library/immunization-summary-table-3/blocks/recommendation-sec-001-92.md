Record (2024, 99: 225-248) • Malaria vaccines should be provided in a 4-dose schedule in children from 5 months of age. • The minimum interval between any doses is 4 weeks; however, to achieve prolonged protection, the fourth dose should be given 6–18 months after the third dose. • To improve coverage, there can be flexibility in the timing of the fourth dose, including by aligning it with vaccines given in the second year of life.
