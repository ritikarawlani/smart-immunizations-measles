Countries may choose to use a one-dose schedule but if they do, they should note that, while such a programme may be sufficient to reduce mortality and severe morbidity from varicella, it is less effective than a 2-dose schedule in preventing virus circulation and occasional outbreaks. • For catch-up vaccination in adolescents and adults, a 2-dose schedule of varicella vaccine should also be used. • Varicella vaccine can be administered concomitantly with other vaccines.
