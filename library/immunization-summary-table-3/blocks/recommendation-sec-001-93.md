Alternatively, because vaccine efficacy is highest in the first months after vaccination, the fourth dose can be given just prior to seasonal peaks in malaria transmission to optimize vaccine efficacy. • A fifth dose, given one year after the fourth dose, may be provided in areas of highly seasonal transmission and may be considered in other areas – depending on a local assessment of feasibility and cost-effectiveness – where a significant malaria risk remains for children. • At the time of vaccine introduction, catch-up vaccination can be considered in children up to 5 years of age, subject to local epidemiology and age of high risk, feasibility, affordability and vaccine availability. • Malaria vaccines may be administered simultaneously with other childhood vaccines.
