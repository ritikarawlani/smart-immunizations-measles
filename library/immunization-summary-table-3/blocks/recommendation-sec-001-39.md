The booster dose should be given at 9–18 months of age, according to programmatic considerations; there is no defined minimum or maximum interval between the primary series and the booster dose. • If the 3p+0 schedule is used, a minimum interval of 4 weeks should be maintained between doses. • Off-label 1p+1 (see position paper for criteria): The first dose of the 1p+1 schedule can be given at ≥6 weeks of age, and the booster can be given at ≥9 months of age. • Interrupted schedules should be resumed without repeating the previous dose. • If a series cannot be completed with the same type of vaccine, the available PCV product should be used.
