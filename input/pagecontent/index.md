### SMART Immunizations — Measles

A measles immunization SMART Guideline authored from the sources attached to
[issue #1](https://github.com/ritikarawlani/smart-immunizations-measles/issues/1):
WHO's immunization summary tables 1–4, the catch-up vaccination guide, the
WHO/UNICEF Joint Reporting Form and the other attachments, ingested into L1,
with the measles recommendations extracted, a Digital Adaptation Kit (L2)
authored from them, and these FHIR artefacts (L3) derived from the DAK.

Everything here is a **draft awaiting review**: no recommendation, DAK
component or FHIR artefact has been reviewed by a person yet. See the
[gap report](gap-report.html).
